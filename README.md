# Learning Compliance RAG

A retrieval-augmented question-answering system for **compliance training knowledge**. It answers questions about regulatory training requirements, internal policies, and course catalogs with paragraph-level citations, and declines when the sources don't support an answer.

> Status: under active development.

## The problem

Organizations that run a learning management system (LMS) have to keep training assignments in sync with regulation. Compliance, L&D, HR, and EHS teams regularly ask questions like:

- *"Which training does a forklift operator need, how often, and which course in our catalog satisfies it?"*
- *"Our internal policy says annual HIPAA refreshers. Is that required by law or is it our own rule?"*
- *"An employee missed their bloodborne-pathogens refresher. What does our escalation SOP say, and what does the regulation require?"*

Answering them means manually cross-referencing three kinds of documents:

1. **Regulations** (e.g. OSHA, HIPAA): the legal source of truth. They are dense, heavily cross-referenced, and cited by paragraph (e.g. `29 CFR 1910.178(l)(4)`).
2. **Internal policies and SOPs**: the organization's interpretation, often stricter than the law, and versioned over time.
3. **Course catalog and LMS configuration**: which course maps to which requirement, recertification intervals, and audience rules.

Mistakes lead to misassigned training, failed audits, and regulatory exposure. This project grounds every answer in the exact regulation paragraph or policy clause so that a reviewer can verify it.

## Corpus (planned)

| Source | Type | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| eCFR API (29 CFR 1910, 45 CFR 164, …) | Real regulations | U.S. public domain |
| Fictional organization "Northwind Manufacturing & Health" | Synthetic policies, SOPs, course catalog, audit memos | Generated by a documented script in `data/` |

No proprietary or customer data is used.
