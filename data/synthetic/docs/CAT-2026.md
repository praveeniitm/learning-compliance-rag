# CAT-2026 Required Training Course Catalog

This catalog lists every course in the Northwind LMS that satisfies a required-training item under POL-CT-001. Durations are seat time. "Recertification" is the interval the LMS uses to create the next assignment; "none" means the course is completed once unless an event triggers retraining.

## Course Details

### EHS-BBP-100 Bloodborne Pathogens Annual

- Requirement satisfied: Bloodborne pathogens (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(2))
- Duration: 45 minutes; modality: e-learning + live Q&A
- Recertification: every 12 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: CLN-RN, CLN-MA, CLN-MGR
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Includes a live Q&A session with a knowledgeable trainer, as required for bloodborne pathogens training. Learners can submit questions in the LMS; e-learning alone is not sufficient.

### PRV-HIP-110 HIPAA Privacy Essentials

- Requirement satisfied: HIPAA privacy (45 CFR 164.530(b))
- Duration: 30 minutes; modality: e-learning
- Recertification: every 12 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: CLN-RN, CLN-MA, CLN-FD, CLN-MGR
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Covers Northwind's own privacy policies and procedures; external HIPAA courses cannot substitute for it.

### PRV-HIP-120 HIPAA Security Awareness

- Requirement satisfied: HIPAA security awareness (45 CFR 164.308(a)(5))
- Duration: 25 minutes; modality: e-learning
- Recertification: every 12 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: CLN-RN, CLN-MA, CLN-FD, CLN-MGR
- Available languages: en, es

### HR-HAR-100 Harassment Prevention for Employees

- Requirement satisfied: Sexual harassment and abusive conduct prevention (Cal. Gov. Code 12950.1)
- Duration: 60 minutes; modality: interactive e-learning
- Recertification: every 24 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: WH-OP-FL, MFG-PA, MFG-MT, CLN-RN, CLN-MA, CLN-FD, CLN-TEL, OFF-GEN
- Available languages: en, es, zh
- Notes: Meets the California 1-hour nonsupervisory requirement, including the abusive conduct and gender identity, gender expression and sexual orientation components.

### HR-HAR-200 Harassment Prevention for Supervisors

- Requirement satisfied: Sexual harassment and abusive conduct prevention (Cal. Gov. Code 12950.1)
- Duration: 120 minutes; modality: interactive e-learning
- Recertification: every 24 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: MFG-SUP, CLN-MGR, OFF-MGR
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Meets the California 2-hour supervisor requirement. May be completed in segments within 30 days, as long as the full 120 minutes are recorded.

### EHS-FL-101 Forklift Operator Fundamentals

- Requirement satisfied: Powered industrial truck (forklift) operation (29 CFR 1910.178(l))
- Duration: 240 minutes; modality: classroom + practical
- Recertification: none; retraining is event-driven
- Assigned to: WH-OP-FL
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Classroom theory plus supervised practical training on the truck type the operator will use. Completion does not authorize operation; the operator must also pass EHS-FL-201.

### EHS-FL-201 Forklift Practical Evaluation

- Requirement satisfied: Powered industrial truck (forklift) operation (29 CFR 1910.178(l))
- Duration: 60 minutes; modality: practical evaluation
- Recertification: every 36 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: WH-OP-FL
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Practical evaluation performed by an EHS-qualified evaluator under SOP-EHS-007. Required before first operation and every 36 months, and after any refresher trigger.

### EHS-FL-210 Forklift Annual Refresher

- Requirement satisfied: Powered industrial truck (forklift) operation (29 CFR 1910.178(l))
- Duration: 30 minutes; modality: e-learning
- Recertification: every 12 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: WH-OP-FL
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Annual online refresher required by POL-CT-001 v3.2. It does not replace the 36-month practical evaluation.

### EHS-RSP-110 Respiratory Protection and Fit Test

- Requirement satisfied: Respiratory protection (29 CFR 1910.134(k) and (f))
- Duration: 90 minutes; modality: classroom + fit test
- Recertification: every 12 months from the last completion
- Assigned to: MFG-MT, CLN-RN
- Available languages: en, es
- Notes: Includes a qualitative or quantitative fit test. Employees must complete the medical evaluation questionnaire before the fit test (SOP-EHS-012).
