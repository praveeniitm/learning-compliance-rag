## Role Notes

### WH-OP-FL Warehouse Operator (Forklift)

Employees in job code WH-OP-FL (Warehouse Operator (Forklift)) at FRE, DAY, REN are assigned: EHS-FL-101 Forklift Operator Fundamentals; EHS-FL-201 Forklift Practical Evaluation; EHS-FL-210 Forklift Annual Refresher; EHS-HC-100 Hazard Communication and GHS Labels; EHS-EAP-100 Site Emergency Action Plan; HR-HAR-100 Harassment Prevention for Employees; ETH-COC-100 Code of Conduct.
Operators may not be issued a forklift badge until EHS-FL-101 and EHS-FL-201 are both complete.

### MFG-PA Production Associate

Employees in job code MFG-PA (Production Associate) at DAY, REN are assigned: EHS-HC-100 Hazard Communication and GHS Labels; EHS-LOTO-050 Lockout/Tagout Awareness; EHS-HCP-100 Hearing Conservation; EHS-EAP-100 Site Emergency Action Plan; HR-HAR-100 Harassment Prevention for Employees; ETH-COC-100 Code of Conduct.

### MFG-MT Maintenance Technician

Employees in job code MFG-MT (Maintenance Technician) at DAY, REN, FRE are assigned: EHS-LOTO-150 Lockout/Tagout for Authorized Employees; EHS-HC-100 Hazard Communication and GHS Labels; EHS-CS-300 Confined Space Entrant; EHS-RSP-110 Respiratory Protection and Fit Test; EHS-HCP-100 Hearing Conservation; EHS-EAP-100 Site Emergency Action Plan; HR-HAR-100 Harassment Prevention for Employees; ETH-COC-100 Code of Conduct.
Confined space (EHS-CS-300) is assigned only to technicians designated as entrants, attendants or entry supervisors on the site confined space roster; the LMS uses the "CS-roster" flag from HRIS.

### MFG-SUP Production Supervisor

Employees in job code MFG-SUP (Production Supervisor) at DAY, REN are assigned: EHS-HC-100 Hazard Communication and GHS Labels; EHS-LOTO-050 Lockout/Tagout Awareness; EHS-HCP-100 Hearing Conservation; EHS-EAP-100 Site Emergency Action Plan; EHS-FE-100 Portable Fire Extinguisher Use; HR-HAR-200 Harassment Prevention for Supervisors; ETH-COC-100 Code of Conduct.
Production supervisors are the designated portable fire extinguisher users for their area.

### CLN-RN Clinic Nurse

## Site-Level Additions

- Employees in the hearing conservation program (exposure at or above 85 dBA TWA) at DAY and REN are assigned EHS-HCP-100 regardless of job code.
- Any employee issued a tight-fitting respirator is assigned EHS-RSP-110 regardless of job code.
