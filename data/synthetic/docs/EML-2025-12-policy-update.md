# Announcement: Compliance Training Policy v3.2 takes effect January 1, 2026

**From:** Marcus Ellery, Chief Compliance Officer
**To:** All people managers
**Date:** 2025-12-01
**Subject:** What changes in required training on January 1

Colleagues,

Version 3.2 of POL-CT-001 takes effect on January 1, 2026. What changes for your teams:

1. **New hires** have 30 days (previously 45) to complete required training. Safety-critical training is still required before the task.
2. **Role changes** now have a 14-day window (previously 30).
3. **HIPAA refreshers** for clinic and PHI-access staff become annual (previously every two years). The first annual assignment will be created for everyone whose last completion is more than 12 months old, due March 31, 2026.
4. **Harassment prevention** extends to all U.S. employees. Employees in Ohio and Nevada will receive HR-HAR-100 or HR-HAR-200 during Q1.
5. **Forklift operators** get a new 30-minute annual online refresher (EHS-FL-210). The 3-year practical evaluation does not change.
6. **Grace period** for annual non-safety-critical items drops from 30 to 15 days.

Questions can go to Compliance Office (compliance@northwind.example).

Marcus
