# AUD-2026-Q2 Internal Audit Memo: Required Training Compliance

To: Marcus Ellery, Chief Compliance Officer; Dana Whitfield, Director of Environmental Health & Safety; Priya Raman, Learning Systems Manager
From: Internal Audit
Date: 2026-07-08
Audit period: 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-30

## Summary

Internal Audit tested required-training assignments and completions for all six sites against POL-CT-001 v3.2, MAT-ROLE-2026 and the LMS completion data. Four findings were raised: three high severity and one medium severity. Overall completion of required training across Northwind was 94.1% (target 98%).

## Findings

### F-1 (high severity, site FCL)

Bloodborne pathogens refresher (EHS-BBP-100) completion at the Fresno clinic was 86% against a 100% target; 43 of 310 staff were overdue, 12 of them by more than 60 days.

Corrective action owner: FCL clinic manager. Target date: 2026-08-15.

Root cause: the clinic onboarded 38 per-diem staff in February 2026 through a staffing agency whose workers were not loaded into HRIS, so the LMS never assigned EHS-BBP-100. Recommended action: load agency staff into HRIS within 2 business days of start, and have the clinic manager confirm BBP completion before scheduling exposure-prone shifts.

### F-2 (high severity, site FRE)

11 forklift operators at the Fresno Distribution Center had a last practical evaluation (EHS-FL-201) older than 36 months, yet their forklift badges were still active.

Corrective action owner: Dana Whitfield. Target date: 2026-07-31.

Root cause: the badge-suspension integration described in SOP-LMS-021 section 3.1 failed silently after the access control system upgrade in November 2025. Recommended action: restore the integration, add a daily reconciliation report of active badges without a current EHS-FL-201, and re-evaluate the 11 operators immediately.

### F-3 (high severity, site CLB)

The Telehealth Coordinator job code (CLN-TEL), created in March 2026, has no HIPAA privacy or security courses in its curriculum. 18 employees handle PHI without assigned HIPAA training.

Corrective action owner: Priya Raman. Target date: 2026-07-15.

Root cause: the new job code was created in HRIS without a change request to Learning Systems. Recommended action: add PRV-HIP-110 and PRV-HIP-120 to CLN-TEL, assign them to the 18 employees with a 14-day due date, and require a curriculum review for every new job code before activation.

### F-4 (medium severity, site ALL)

Harassment prevention due dates for 214 employees transferred from non-California sites still followed the pre-2026 (v3.1) scope and were not assigned at all.

Corrective action owner: HR Operations. Target date: 2026-09-30.

Root cause: the v3.2 harassment scope change was implemented for new hires but not back-filled for existing employees outside California, New York, Illinois and Connecticut. Recommended action: run a one-time assignment for all U.S. employees without an HR-HAR-100 or HR-HAR-200 completion in the last 24 months.

## Management Response

EHS and Learning Systems accepted all findings. The CLN-TEL dynamic group rule was corrected on 2026-07-10 (SOP-LMS-014 5.2).
