# SOP-EHS-012 Respiratory Protection Training and Fit Testing

## 1. Scope

Applies to employees required to wear respirators, including maintenance technicians performing paint booth and solvent cleaning work and clinic nurses who wear N95 filtering facepiece respirators for airborne-isolation patients. Governing regulation: 29 CFR 1910.134.

## 2. Sequence Before First Use

2.1 Medical evaluation: the employee completes the confidential medical questionnaire, reviewed by the occupational health physician at the Columbus or Fresno clinic. No fit test is performed until medical clearance is recorded.

2.2 Training: the employee completes EHS-RSP-110, which covers why the respirator is necessary, its limitations, how to inspect, don and doff it, how to perform a user seal check, and how to recognize medical signs that may limit its use.

2.3 Fit test: a qualitative (Bitrex or saccharin) or quantitative fit test is performed on the exact make, model, style and size of respirator the employee will use.

## 3. Annual Requirements

3.1 Training and fit testing are repeated every 12 months. The LMS tracks both as components of EHS-RSP-110; the course is not complete until the fit test result is recorded.

3.2 A new fit test is also required whenever a different facepiece (size, style, model or make) is used, or when the employee reports, or a supervisor or clinician observes, changes in physical condition that could affect fit (for example significant weight change, facial scarring, or dental changes).

## 4. Retraining Outside the Annual Cycle

4.1 Retraining is required when changes in the workplace or the type of respirator make previous training obsolete, when the employee's knowledge or use of the respirator indicates they have not retained the required understanding, or when any other situation arises in which retraining appears necessary.

## 5. Voluntary Use

5.1 Employees who voluntarily wear filtering facepieces (dust masks) where respirators are not required receive the Appendix D information sheet from 29 CFR 1910.134; they are not assigned EHS-RSP-110.
