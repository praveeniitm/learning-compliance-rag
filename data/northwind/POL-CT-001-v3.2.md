# POL-CT-001 Compliance Training Policy (Version 3.2)

Effective date: 2026-01-01. Approved by: Marcus Ellery, Chief Compliance Officer.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Northwind Manufacturing & Health ("Northwind") identifies, assigns, tracks and documents training that is required by law, by regulation, or by Northwind's own risk decisions. It applies to all training delivered through the Northwind learning management system (LMS).

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to all 4800 employees across 6 locations, including temporary employees engaged for more than 30 days and contractors who operate Northwind equipment or access protected health information (PHI).

2.2 Sites in scope: Dayton Assembly Plant (DAY, OH); Fresno Distribution Center (FRE, CA); Reno Components Plant (REN, NV); Fresno Occupational Health Clinic (FCL, CA); Columbus Occupational Health Clinic (CLB, OH); Columbus Headquarters (HQ, OH).

## 3. Definitions

3.1 **Required training** means a course or evaluation that an employee must complete because of a regulation, a statute, or this policy.

3.2 **Safety-critical training** means required training without which the employee must not perform the related task, for example operating a forklift, entering a permit-required confined space, or performing tasks with occupational exposure to blood.

3.3 **Recertification interval** means the maximum time between two completions of the same requirement. Intervals are measured from the completion date of the previous completion, not from the original assignment date.

3.4 **Grace period** means the number of days after the due date during which an overdue item is escalated but not yet treated as a policy violation.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 The **Compliance Office** owns this policy, interprets regulatory requirements, and resolves conflicts between sources (see Section 5).

4.2 **Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)**, led by Dana Whitfield, Director of Environmental Health & Safety, owns the content and evaluation standards of all safety training and approves external safety training equivalency.

4.3 **Learning Systems**, led by Priya Raman, Learning Systems Manager, configures assignment rules, recurrence and escalation in the LMS in accordance with SOP-LMS-014 and SOP-LMS-021.

4.4 **Managers** ensure that their direct reports complete required training on time and do not assign safety-critical tasks to employees whose training is not current.

## 5. Requirement Sources and Precedence

5.1 Where a regulation and this policy both address the same training, the **stricter** requirement applies. This policy never relaxes a legal requirement.

5.2 Order of authority: (1) statute or regulation; (2) this policy; (3) standard operating procedures (SOPs), the course catalog and the role curriculum matrix; (4) guidance, FAQs and communications. Informal communications such as e-mails do not change a requirement.

5.3 Where two sources conflict, or a requirement is unclear, the Learning Systems team must not configure a rule until the Compliance Office has issued a written interpretation.

## 6. Assignment Windows

6.1 **New hires** must complete all required non-safety-critical training within 30 calendar days of their start date.

6.2 **Safety-critical training** must be completed before the employee performs the related task, regardless of the 30-day window.

6.3 **Role or site changes** that add requirements must be completed within 14 calendar days of the effective date of the change, or before the related task if safety-critical.

6.4 **Newly promoted supervisors** must complete supervisor-level harassment prevention training within 30 days of the promotion effective date.

## 7. Recertification Intervals

7.1 The following intervals apply. The "Legal basis" column is informative; the "Northwind rule" column is what the LMS enforces.

| Topic | Legal basis | Northwind rule |
|---|---|---|
| Bloodborne pathogens | 29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(2) | at initial assignment (before any task with occupational exposure) and every 12 months |
| HIPAA privacy | 45 CFR 164.530(b) | within 30 days of hire and every 12 months |
| HIPAA security awareness | 45 CFR 164.308(a)(5) | within 30 days of hire and every 12 months |
| Sexual harassment and abusive conduct prevention | Cal. Gov. Code 12950.1 | all U.S. employees: every 24 months; new hires within 30 days |
| Powered industrial truck (forklift) operation | 29 CFR 1910.178(l) | initial training and practical evaluation before operating; practical re-evaluation every 36 months; annual online refresher module |
| Respiratory protection | 29 CFR 1910.134(k) and (f) | training and fit test before first use and every 12 months |
| Lockout/tagout (control of hazardous energy) | 29 CFR 1910.147(c)(7) | authorized employees every 24 months plus event-driven retraining; affected employees awareness at hire |
| Hazard communication (GHS) | 29 CFR 1910.1200(h) | at initial assignment, when a new hazard is introduced, and every 36 months |
| Portable fire extinguisher use | 29 CFR 1910.157(g) | designated extinguisher users at hire and every 12 months |
| Hearing conservation | 29 CFR 1910.95(k) | every 12 months for employees at or above 85 dBA TWA |
| Permit-required confined spaces | 29 CFR 1910.146(g) | before first assignment and every 24 months |
| Emergency action plan | 29 CFR 1910.38(e) | at hire, on site transfer, and when the site plan changes |
| Code of conduct | none (internal requirement) | all employees at hire and every 12 months |

7.2 **HIPAA refresher.** Workforce members at clinics, and any other employee with access to PHI, complete HIPAA privacy and security training annual. The HIPAA rules require training on joining the workforce and after material changes but do not set a fixed refresher interval; the annual refresher is a Northwind requirement adopted after the 2025 risk assessment.

7.3 **Harassment prevention scope.** Harassment and abusive conduct prevention training applies to all U.S. employees. Supervisors take the 2-hour supervisor course (HR-HAR-200); all other employees take the 1-hour course (HR-HAR-100). Applying the California standard to every U.S. employee simplifies administration for employees who transfer between states.

7.4 **Early completion.** An employee may complete a recurring item up to 60 days before its due date. The next due date is then calculated from the new completion date (see 3.3).

## 8. Due Dates, Grace Period and Overdue Training

8.1 Annual, non-safety-critical items have a grace period of 15 calendar days after the due date.

8.2 Safety-critical items have **no grace period**. On the due date, the manager must remove the employee from the related task until the training is complete, and the related equipment authorization (for example the forklift badge) is suspended.

8.3 Escalation steps for overdue items are defined in SOP-LMS-021.

## 9. Exemptions and Equivalency

9.1 Training completed with another employer or provider may satisfy a requirement if it covered the same content, was completed within the applicable recertification interval, and is approved under SOP-LMS-030.

9.2 Site-specific elements (for example the emergency action plan, site-specific forklift conditions, or Northwind's own HIPAA policies and procedures) can never be satisfied by external training.

## 10. Records

10.1 Training records are retained for 5 years after completion unless a longer period applies.

10.2 Where a regulation specifies retention, the longer period applies. For example, bloodborne pathogens training records must be kept for at least 3 years (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(2)(ii)), and HIPAA documentation for 6 years (45 CFR 164.530(j)); Northwind retains HIPAA training records for 6 years.

10.3 Forklift operator certifications must record the operator name, training date, evaluation date and the identity of the trainer or evaluator (29 CFR 1910.178(l)(6)).
