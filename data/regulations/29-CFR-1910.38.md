# 29 CFR 1910.38 Emergency action plans.

Nomenclature changes to part 1910 appear at 84 FR 21597, May 14, 2019.

## 29 CFR 1910.38(a)
[29 CFR 1910.38(a)] (a) Application. An employer must have an emergency action plan whenever an OSHA standard in this part requires one. The requirements in this section apply to each such emergency action plan.

## 29 CFR 1910.38(b)
[29 CFR 1910.38(b)] (b) Written and oral emergency action plans. An emergency action plan must be in writing, kept in the workplace, and available to employees for review. However, an employer with 10 or fewer employees may communicate the plan orally to employees.

## 29 CFR 1910.38(c)
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)] (c) Minimum elements of an emergency action plan. An emergency action plan must include at a minimum:
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)(1)] (1) Procedures for reporting a fire or other emergency;
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)(2)] (2) Procedures for emergency evacuation, including type of evacuation and exit route assignments;
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)(3)] (3) Procedures to be followed by employees who remain to operate critical plant operations before they evacuate;
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)(4)] (4) Procedures to account for all employees after evacuation;
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)(5)] (5) Procedures to be followed by employees performing rescue or medical duties; and
[29 CFR 1910.38(c)(6)] (6) The name or job title of every employee who may be contacted by employees who need more information about the plan or an explanation of their duties under the plan.

## 29 CFR 1910.38(d)
[29 CFR 1910.38(d)] (d) Employee alarm system. An employer must have and maintain an employee alarm system. The employee alarm system must use a distinctive signal for each purpose and comply with the requirements in § 1910.165.

## 29 CFR 1910.38(e)
[29 CFR 1910.38(e)] (e) Training. An employer must designate and train employees to assist in a safe and orderly evacuation of other employees.

## 29 CFR 1910.38(f)
[29 CFR 1910.38(f)] (f) Review of emergency action plan. An employer must review the emergency action plan with each employee covered by the plan:
[29 CFR 1910.38(f)(1)] (1) When the plan is developed or the employee is assigned initially to a job;
[29 CFR 1910.38(f)(2)] (2) When the employee's responsibilities under the plan change; and
[29 CFR 1910.38(f)(3)] (3) When the plan is changed.
